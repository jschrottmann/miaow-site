Few DJs make their own records. Miaow does both.

Miaow is a Manila-based DJ and producer with roots in ballet, a UP Fine Arts degree and a past life as a model. She came up through the battle circuit, where her quick paws and catlike reflexes let her move between bass, commercial open format, house, and bigroom without missing a beat.

In a scene where few performing DJs also make their own records, Miaow does both. She has released on Warner Music Philippines, lands regularly on New Music Friday Philippines, and has collaborated with some of OPM’s most respected names on genre-bending records that fuse bass music with alternative, pop and R&B.

Her wins speak for themselves: the IMS Ibiza Future Talent Grand Prix, an opening slot for Pete Tong at Tantra Ibiza, and most recently a remix contest win with UK label Cr2 Records. Next up is her new sound: global dance music that carries Filipino instrumentation and flavour onto international dancefloors.

“I’ve done what I can at home. Now, with a new sound, this kitty’s ready to make footprints globally.”MIAOW
